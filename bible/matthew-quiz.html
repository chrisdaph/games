About the Matthew Bible Quiz

This Bible quiz turns ten favourite moments from the book of Matthew into a fun, gentle game for young children. Every question comes with a big picture, a short sentence, and three picture answers to choose from, so kids ages 5 to 7 who are just learning to read can play along with a grown-up or older sibling. There is no pressure to get it right the first time, a friendly nudge lets them try again.

How to Play

  1. Read the question, or have a grown-up read it aloud.
  2. Tap the picture answer you think is right.
  3. A correct answer earns a star and moves you to the next question.
  4. Finish all ten questions to see your star rating and a recap of every answer!

Bible Stories in This Quiz

The quiz covers ten moments from Matthew's Gospel: the wise men following the star, Jesus' baptism, the twelve disciples, the Lord's Prayer, the parable of the mustard seed, feeding the 5,000, Palm Sunday, the Last Supper, and the resurrection.

Yes. It plays free in any web browser with nothing to download or install.

It is designed for kids ages 5 to 7, with short questions, picture answers, and encouraging feedback.

Ten questions from the book of Matthew, including the wise men, Jesus' baptism, the Lord's Prayer, feeding the 5,000, Palm Sunday, and the resurrection.
