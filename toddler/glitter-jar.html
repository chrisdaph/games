About Glitter Jar

Glitter Jar gives toddlers a calm, visual way to settle down. Press the button to give the jar a gentle shake, then watch the sparkles swirl up and slowly drift back down. There is no rushing and no score to lose, just a soothing moment to breathe and watch.

How to Play

  1. Press "Shake the Jar" to start.
  2. Watch the glitter swirl up in a burst of color.
  3. Take slow breaths while it drifts back down to rest.
  4. Repeat four times to finish, then rest and celebrate!

Why a Glitter Jar Helps

Many families keep a real glitter jar at home, a simple bottle of water and glitter that turns big feelings into a calm, watchable moment. Following the glitter with your eyes as it settles gives little minds something gentle to focus on, making it easier to slow down and calm the body.
